University of South Asia

University of South Asia is the University for Generation Bangladesh who aspires for an inclusive Bangladesh. It connects students and lets the network they build here support their career.

Located in Amin Bazar, Savar, Dhaka, the University attracts students and staff from various backgrounds through its commitment to innovation and excellence. The campus offers an environment for an academic institution.
